# Historical — Sudarshan Task Force (STF) Pack 01

This folder is the original product pack for the product that became **FlowHRMS by Flowacord** (rebranded 14 September 2026). It is kept unchanged as the record of the founding decisions; every name, logo and colour in it refers to the retired STF brand. The living versions of these documents are under `docs/md/`, which carry the FlowHRMS name.
