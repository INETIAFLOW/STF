# Sudarshan Task Force - Product Vision

Version: 0.1  |  Date: 07 August 2026  |  Status: Draft for approval

## Vision
Make workforce operations simple enough for a busy SME owner to run from a phone, while keeping the records reliable enough for payroll, accountability, and growth.

## Mission
Bring attendance, leave, payroll inputs, task ownership, daily reporting, and employee records into one configurable system that works for office, warehouse, dispatch, delivery, and field teams.

## The problem
Small businesses commonly operate through calls, WhatsApp, paper registers, spreadsheets, and memory. Attendance is disputed, task follow-up is inconsistent, leave reaches payroll late, and owners lack one daily view of operations.

## The promise
STF turns daily work into clear evidence: check-in with time and permitted location; approved exceptions; assigned tasks with proof; policies that visibly affect payroll; and concise daily summaries for decision makers.

## Positioning
STF is not corporate HR software made smaller. It is a configurable operating tool for practical businesses where presence, field movement, tasks, and daily accountability matter.

## Principles of value
Clarity over complexity. Evidence over assumptions. Configuration over custom development. Employee dignity over surveillance theatre. Reviewable payroll over opaque totals. Useful daily summaries over vanity dashboards.

## First market focus
Indian SMEs with 10-500 employees, beginning with hardware/trading, warehouse, dispatch, delivery, and service operations. Build legal and language localization as configured requirements, not assumptions.

## Give this to Claude
Use this vision to guide naming, visual direction, landing-page messaging, and mobile flows. The product should feel capable, dependable, direct, and respectful - not overly corporate or playful.
