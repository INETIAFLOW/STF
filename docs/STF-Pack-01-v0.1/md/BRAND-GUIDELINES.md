# Sudarshan Task Force - Brand Guidelines

Version: 0.1  |  Date: 07 August 2026  |  Status: Draft for approval

## Brand core
Name: Sudarshan Task Force. Short form: STF. Suggested descriptor: Workforce, Tasks, Attendance, Payroll. Brand idea: clear direction and disciplined follow-through.

## Personality
Practical, calm, accountable, modern, and supportive. STF speaks to business owners and workers with equal respect. Avoid military aggression, surveillance language, and empty enterprise jargon.

## Voice
Use short active sentences: "Check in", "Task due today", "Leave approved", "Review payroll". Explain consequences before an action: "This will mark a late arrival of 18 minutes." Errors must say what happened and what the user can do next.

## Visual direction
Use a clean, high-contrast mobile interface with generous spacing and large touch targets. The visual system should communicate trust and operational clarity. Start with a deep navy or indigo foundation, a confident warm accent, neutral surfaces, and accessible status colors. Final color tokens, typography, icon set, and logo are design-phase decisions and must be documented before code.

## Logo brief
Create a compact symbol plus wordmark that works in an app icon, browser tab, login screen, payslip, and monochrome print. Explore an abstract circular direction/coordination motif; do not use weapons, literal surveillance pins, or copied religious/political imagery. Test at 16px, light/dark surfaces, and one color.

## Photography and illustration
Prefer authentic Indian SME work contexts: counters, warehouses, dispatch, delivery, workshops, and field visits. Show people with dignity. Use illustration only to clarify empty, loading, and onboarding states.

## Give this to Claude
Create three distinct STF brand directions using this brief. For each, show logo, app icon, token palette, typography, dashboard sample, mobile check-in sample, and landing-page hero. Keep all directions practical and accessible.
