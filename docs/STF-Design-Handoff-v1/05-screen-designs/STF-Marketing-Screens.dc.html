Five things that decide your day
Not a smaller version of corporate HR software. A working tool for businesses where presence, movement and follow-through are the job.
Attendance
One large Check In. Time and permitted location recorded. Exceptions go to a manager, not a register.
Task ownership
Assign with a priority, a due time and a proof requirement. See what was actually delivered.
Leave approval
Requests reach the right manager the same minute, with the payroll effect shown before the decision.
Payroll visibility
Every salary figure shows the attendance, leave and adjustments behind it. Review before you pay.
Accountability
Who decided what, when, and why — kept as a record you can look up months later.
RESPECTFUL BY DESIGN
Evidence, not surveillance
Location is captured at check-in and check-out — not continuously. Employees see exactly what was recorded about them, in the same words their manager sees. Working outside a branch is a normal event to confirm, not a fault to catch.
✓
No continuous tracking, face recognition or biometric hardware
✓
Every approval keeps the person, the time and the reason
✓
Sensitive data is behind separate permissions, and access is logged
